THE IDEA

Students learn
by leading.

KAIRO is a college club run by the students, for the students. Learning about leadership principles in lectures and practically applying them, members practice by planning events, leading teams and shipping real projects on campus.

Why it matters: employers and ideas reward people who can organise others and finish things. KAIRO is where you build that proof — one project at a time.
